## What the planning conversation replaced

The Portugal program was superseded, not adapted. It was ninety minutes shorter,
had a different co-facilitator, and was built for four people. Three things in
it read as reusable and are not:

- **The five guiding principles.** The 16 August evening names four, and they
  are differently worded. Welcome everything and trust your experience; present
  moment focus and not going into stories; own your experience, reveal it, share
  the impact; do not fix, change, or rescue.
- **The Wink** and **the Noticing Game.** Neither is in the current evening. The
  relational work is now two pair rounds built on sensation, feeling and story,
  then threes, then one circle.

The exercise material carries over. The structure and the framing do not. Start
from `../session-plan.md`, not from here.
