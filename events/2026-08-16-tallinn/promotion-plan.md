# Promotion plan — Circling & Authentic Relating, 16 August 2026

**Status:** cover and description are done and the Facebook event is live.
Everything below is about getting people into the room. Revised 1 August, when
the target came down to 8 to 12 people and ticketing was confirmed.

- Event: https://www.facebook.com/events/1947390679313071
- Page: https://www.facebook.com/SoulFamilyEvents
- Tickets: €20 via Fienta, free option for anyone for whom money is the obstacle.
- 15 days from 1 August to the event on Sunday 16 August, 16:30.

## Goal

**8 to 12 people in the room.** Confirmed 1 August.

Ticketing: **€20 through Fienta**, with an option to come for free if money is
the obstacle. That combination is close to ideal for a room this size. The
ticket converts intention into commitment, and the free option means nobody who
belongs in the circle is filtered out by price.

The funnel, which is much gentler than it looks:

- Registered attendees show up at roughly **80 to 90 percent** when they paid,
  and somewhat lower on a free ticket where nothing is at stake. Call the blend
  **80 percent**.
- To seat 10, you need about **13 registrations**. To be sure of 8, about 10.
- Aim for **14**. Slightly over, because a couple will fall away in the last
  week and a room of 12 is still a good room.

Fourteen registrations is a small number. It is roughly what two people get from
one focused afternoon of personal messages, and it means almost everything in
the wide-reach half of this plan is now optional. Do not spend money on this.

Items 1 and 2 fill the room on their own. Items 3 to 5 are the margin of safety.
Item 7 was a "no" in the first draft of this plan, on the assumption that a warm
network was already converting. On 1 August it turned out that every Going and
Interested on the event was an organizer, so there was nothing warm to convert
and the ads became worth running. They are still the smaller half.

A note on the cap. Twelve is a real limit, not a marketing device, so it is
honest to say plainly: "the room holds about twelve." State it as a fact once,
in the event and in the messages. Don't count down, don't dress it as scarcity.

### 2 to 4 Aug — the personal messages

This is the part that actually fills the room, so protect the time for it.

- [ ] Send individual messages. Not a forward, not a broadcast. Template in

Expect roughly a third to say yes. Twenty-eight messages, nine or ten yeses.
That is most of a room of twelve from one afternoon, which is why this beat is
the plan rather than one item in it.

## Paid ads

**Decision, 1 August: run them, at €80 of a €100 ceiling.**

This reverses the earlier "don't". That advice assumed a warm network was
already converting. It isn't. Checked on 1 August the event shows four Going,
and all four are you: two organisers, the page itself, and one person who has
said they won't actually come. External signups are **zero**. Fifteen days out
with an empty room, the calculation changes, and Dmitrijs is willing to spend.

### Five things to fix before spending a cent

Every one of these silently wastes the budget:

1. **The 4:30 AM start time.** Paying to send people to an event that tells
   them to arrive at half four in the morning.
2. **The ad account.** Diagnosed 1 August. The account is
   **215940746080050**, Dmitrijs's own, sitting in "Other assets" rather than in
   a portfolio. Two separate things are wrong with it and both must be fixed:
   - **An account spending limit of €10, already fully spent** since 3 December.
     This is the hard stop. "Reset amount spent" only zeroes the counter against
     the same €10 cap, so it would die again after the first day. Remove the
     limit, or raise it to €150.
   - **No valid payment method**, which is what produced "your ad account is
     disabled" and, in the boost flow, the misleading "you do not have
     permissions to update the ad account".

   The **Soulfull Events** portfolio exists but contains **0 ad accounts**, so
   it cannot pay for anything. A portfolio is a container, not an ad account.
   Note the typo in its name against the Page's "Soulful events".

   **EcoLogic Culture is not a separate business here.** It does not appear in
   the portfolio list, so it is almost certainly a stale payer and beneficiary
   entry saved on this ad account. It is an editable field, not an ownership
   problem. Change it before running anything.
3. **The ad text.** The boost flow has pre-filled the entire event description.
   Facebook cuts to roughly 125 characters before "See more", so all of it past
   the first line is wasted. Replace with variant A from `promo-copy.md`.
4. **Say that the evening is held in English.** Confirmed 1 August: English
   only, no Russian, no Estonian. Nowhere on the event does it currently say so.
   In a city where a large share of people are more comfortable in Russian or
   Estonian, leaving it unsaid sells a seat to someone who then finds out on
   arrival. One plain line in the description.
5. **Some genuine social proof.** Cold traffic landing on a four-Going event
   converts badly. Send the personal messages first and let two or three days
   of real names accumulate, then start the ads. Ads on 4 August, not today.

### The arithmetic, using Meta's own numbers

The boost preview quotes 583 to 1,800 impressions per day for €2, so roughly
€1.70 CPM. Estonia is cheap. €80 over twelve days is therefore something like
45,000 to 90,000 impressions and, at the €1-per-click Meta is estimating,
somewhere near 80 clicks. Cold clicks to a €20 vulnerable evening convert
poorly, call it 3 to 8 percent, so **2 to 6 people through the door**.

That is a real dent in a room of twelve, and the residue has value beyond this
event: page followers, an Interested list, and a warm pool to retarget for the
next one. It does not replace the personal messages, which remain the thing
that actually fills the room.

### Setup

**One boost, in English, €60, running 4 to 15 August.** Hold **€20 back as a
reserve** for 9 August.

An earlier draft of this section split the budget across an English and a
Russian ad. That is now wrong: the evening is held in English only, so a
Russian ad would be selling a seat under false pretences. And with the language
split gone there is no second audience worth separating. At roughly €1.70 CPM
you are buying about 80 clicks in total; divide that across two ad sets and
neither half is large enough to tell you anything. One boost, one audience,
two or three images, and let Meta pick the image. That is the only comparison
this budget can actually resolve.

**Published 1 August.** What actually went live, which differs from the draft
above in a few places and those differences are deliberate:

- **Location: Tallinn +10 mi, Harju County.** Optimize locations off. The boost
  flow defaults to "Estonia"; that default spends your money on Tartu and
  Pärnu, and nobody drives from either for a Sunday evening.
- **Age 22 to 45.**
- **Interests: Meditations, Personal development, Self care, Psychology,
  Emotional intelligence, Yoga.**
- **Advantage+ audience: On.** The draft above said turn it off. That was based
  on a wrong premise: Advantage+ does not override location, which stays a hard
  constraint, it only expands past age, gender and interests. With the
  geography locked, leaving it on is better than hand-picking, because the
  interests act as a seed Meta can escape once it finds who actually responds.
  A person with no wellness interests at all may want this evening most; a hard
  interest fence is the one thing that guarantees never reaching them.
- **The seed has to be clean.** The first attempt also carried
  `Employers: Meditation`, `Job title: Yoga Teacher / Personal Development
  Mentor / Holistic Health Practitioner` and `Field of study: Spirituality`.
  Detailed targeting rows are joined with OR, so these did not narrow anything,
  they added people. Worse, with Advantage+ on they told Meta to expand toward
  wellness professionals, who are the one group already reachable for free.
- **€60 total over 15 days**, about €4 a day. €2 a day was the first attempt and
  it is too thin to deliver evenly.
- **Advertiser and Payer: Soulful Events.**
- **Destination: the Facebook event**, not the Fienta page. Cold traffic needs
  the cover, the description and the social proof before a checkout screen.
- **Creative: one image.** The boost flow takes a single image and does not
  rotate, so the plan to test two or three is not available. It is
  `brand/out/ad-wide-01.png`, rendered from
  `brand/covers/ad-wide-01.html` with `./render.sh ads`: the collage circle
  left, "Meet people, for real this time" right, at 1920×1005, which is the
  1.91:1 landscape ratio Facebook wants and downscales cleanly to 1200×628.

Meta's own estimate at these settings was 4,000 to 7,500 impressions a day and
31 link clicks a day. Those are daily, not totals, and they lean optimistic.
Half of it, so 200 to 250 clicks across the run, is the number to plan against.

### Review points

- **9 August.** If the boost is producing event responses at a sane cost, add
  the €20 reserve to it. If it isn't, keep the €20 and spend it on nothing.
  Change nothing else either way; a bad day is noise at this volume, not signal.
- **11 August.** If the ads have produced event responses but no registrations,
  the barrier is trust, not price. Switch the rest of the budget to a Messages
  ad, since the description already asks people to write. A conversation
  converts this kind of evening far better than a checkout page does.
- Stop all spend on 15 August. Never run an event ad into the event day.

### Note on the advertiser disclosure

The EU beneficiary and payer fields are filled with **EcoLogic Culture**, which
is not a name anyone associates with this event. Meta shows this only in the Ad
Library and not on the ad, so it will not confuse anyone reading it, but check
it is the entity you actually want on public record as paying.

## If you're behind at day 10

If on 11 August you have fewer than **six registrations**, the useful responses
in order:

1. Another 15 personal messages. Always this first.
2. Ask three people who are already coming to each personally invite one friend.
   A direct ask converts, a share does not.
3. Say the free option louder. If people are hesitating at €20 and you haven't
   made the no-money route obvious, that's the cheapest fix available.
4. Post in two more communities, and ask Üks Maja again.

Note that "behind" here means six, not sixteen. Resist the urge to panic-widen
the net; a circle of eight people who were personally invited is a better
evening than twelve strangers pulled in from an ad.

What not to do: countdown-timer urgency, "only 3 spots left" when there aren't,
or anything that manufactures pressure. It would misrepresent a slow, sincere
evening and it would put off exactly the people who belong in the room.
