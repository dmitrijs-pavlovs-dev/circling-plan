# Body scan source scripts — what was studied and what was taken

Downloaded 15 August 2026 while revising the grounding meditation script in
`session-plan.md` / `program.html`. The PDFs in this folder are copyrighted
third-party material, kept locally for reference and **git-ignored** so they
are not republished on the public repo. This file records what each source is
and which craft elements were folded into our script.

## Sources

| File | Source | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `kabat-zinn-palouse.pdf` | Jon Kabat-Zinn, *Coming to Our Senses* excerpt via palousemindfulness.com | Essay on the MBSR body scan, the classic toes-upward sweep order, vocabulary lists of sensations, emotions and thoughts, and a safety note for overwhelm |
| `ucla-marc.pdf` | UCLA Mindful Awareness Research Center | Short (2:44) seated scan, the "notice, then let it soften" pattern |
| `greater-good.pdf` | Greater Good in Action, UC Berkeley (based on the UCLA scan) | Adds orienting to the room at the start, "if you cannot feel some areas, that is OK", and a gradual return (soft gaze, rotating the head) |
| `wellness-society.pdf` | The Wellness Society | Fully scripted with [brief pause] / [prolonged pause] markers, feet-to-head order, "making room for any sensations, leaning into them" |
| `ecampus-ontario.pdf` | eCampus Ontario pressbook, MBSR-style transcript | The richest craft: attention moves on the out-breath, body parts "dissolve in awareness", "there is no right way to feel", the wandering-mind absolution, breathing into body parts, "coming home to the body" ending |

Also reviewed via web summaries (scripts not reproducible): The Daily
Meditation (crown-down then reverse), Mindful Zen, Therapist Aid (six-and-a-half
minutes with suggested pauses), and the structure of Kristin Neff's
Compassionate Body Scan (gratitude toward each part; audio only). Our own
June 2026 Portugal script (`archive/2026-06-portugal-session-v2.md`) already
carried the self-compassion flavor.

## What was folded into our script

1. **Transitions ride the out-breath** (eCampus Ontario): "a slightly deeper
   breath, and as it leaves, let the attention rise into…" Gives the guide a
   natural pacing device.
2. **Wandering-mind absolution** (eCampus Ontario): nothing has gone wrong,
   that is what minds do, walk it back gently. Placed at the belly, roughly
   halfway, where minds actually wander.
3. **"There is no right way to feel"** and blank areas count (eCampus
   Ontario, Greater Good), merged with our existing "nothing much is also an
   answer".
4. **Whole-body breath before the close** (eCampus Ontario, Kabat-Zinn).
5. **Gentle return** (Greater Good): eyes open without landing on anything,
   letting the room arrive slowly.
6. **Opt-out permission up front** (Kabat-Zinn's safety note, adapted): eyes
   can stay open or reopen at any point. Matters in a room of strangers.

## What was deliberately not taken

- The 45-minute MBSR pacing and left-side/right-side leg-by-leg sweep: too
  long for a 10-minute beat that hands over from talking to moving.
- Gratitude-toward-the-body framing (Neff, our Portugal script): lovely, but
  the current evening keeps this beat neutral and soft; the depth belongs
  later.
- "Notice that you are safe" (Greater Good): tells people what they feel,
  which breaks our voice rules. Replaced with the permission to open the eyes.
